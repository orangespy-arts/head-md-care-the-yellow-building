# Intentions
Time:05-07

It is the last day of the old yellow building. Tomorrow, it will be demolished by greedy landlords. Augustin, who has spent almost his entire life there, takes care of his home one final time, even knowing that everything will disappear the next day. He repairs the floor, paints the walls, cleans the rooms, arranges flowers… before closes the door and leaves forever, he realizes that everyone is doing the same thing.  

**Intention:**

How can we convey to the public, by showcasing this story of this building, that it deserves to be preserved and properly maintained?

**Brainstorms:**

Like a walk/ballad/hike in erased/fading memories,

struggling/fight to hold these memories together,

watching the memories disappear,

find an object (go through all scene in this room/building),

different usages/stories with same object when people are in different ages,

different perspective to see this building in different ages,

need to do so many things in a life routine but the building is the center of this person’s life.

These memories slowly re-surface : mark height on the wall, oil dirt on kitchen’s wall, scratch on the wood floor from the moving sofa, kids drawing on the wall, a picture hiding behind a closet, a lost letter or coffee stains on the wood floor hidden . under the carpet….

**MainStory**

Augustin (all memories in one persona ((with no genders, no age)) opens the door and steps into his apartment. She/He looks around his apartment, the home she/he’s lived in all his life. She/He examines certain objects more closely and remembers some memories ( fuzzy and waiting to be pieced together )

**Other Story1:**

In the morning, Augustin opened the window and let the fresh air in, this is the last day before this building was demolished. He turned on the gas and made breakfast with fried egg and bread for himself, which made him think about the stove. When he was 6 years old, his mom taught him how to make the fried egg, and he lived in this building at that time.

**Other Story2:**

Augustin had a vine plant growing on his wall in his room. This was the last day before the building was demolished. He was going to move, but this plant cannot be moved, because it grows here, blend into the wall as a part of this building. This plant is like him, who grew up here, whose roots grew inside this building.

**Title:**

The Yellow Building

The yellow Building, where I live,

The yellow Building, where i lived,

The yellow Building, where will I live ?

L’Immeuble Jaune

L’immeuble jaune, ou je vis,

L’immeuble jaune, ou je vivais,

L’immeuble jaune, ou vais-je vivre ?

**Context:**

An old yellow building will be demolished for reconstruction, yet it holds countless memories of life **and the people living inside have nowhere else to go without it.

**Persona:**

An old person spent their whole life in this yellow building, with love and shaped by shared moments with neighbors.

**Plot:**

If this yellow building is demolished, what will happen to the memories of the people living here? Are old rituals the solution?
